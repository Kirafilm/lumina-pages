Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This policy describes how Lumina (“we”) handles information when you use the app.

1. Information we collect

Lumina does not require an account, does not include analytics or advertising, and does not upload your photos.

Files you choose

  • Photos and folders you import are read on your Mac so the app can build a catalog, show previews, and export new files.
  • Edit settings, ratings, labels, and collections are stored in the local catalog on your Mac.

2. How we use information

  • To show and organize the photos you import
  • To remember non-destructive edits in the catalog
  • To write exported copies to a folder you choose

3. Storage

Processing stays on your Mac. We do not operate an account system and we do not send your photos to our servers.

4. Third parties

Lumina does not include analytics or advertising SDKs. Image processing uses macOS frameworks on your Mac.

5. Children’s privacy

Lumina is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will change when we do.

7. Contact
